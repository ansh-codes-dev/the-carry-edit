The Carry Edit respects your privacy. This website is a lightweight static editorial lookbook. We do not require user accounts, do not run registration forms, do not process payments, and do not collect or sell personal information directly on this site.

If you choose to contact us via email at thecarryedit.help@gmail.com, we only use your email address and message content to reply to your inquiry. We never share or sell email addresses to third parties.

The Carry Edit is a participant in the Amazon Associates Program. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Once you leave our website and land on Amazon, your browsing and purchase activity is governed by Amazon's own Privacy Notice and Conditions of Use.

We also load web typography via Google Fonts for consistent readability.

5. Third-Party Links

Our pages link to external retail websites (primarily Amazon). We are not responsible for the privacy practices, product availability, or content of external websites.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email us at thecarryedit.help@gmail.com.
